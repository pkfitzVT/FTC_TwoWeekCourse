## Student-Led Discussion

Prompt: How should an engineering team make room for different kinds of contributors?

## Team Task

Students identify strengths, possible roles, and team behaviors that support participation. They continue demo robot rotations with different partners when possible, focusing on chassis geometry, material choices, motor placement, omni wheel placement, shooter design, hopper design, trigger reliability, operator roles, and what they might borrow, modify, or avoid.

## Build / Program / Test Time

Students operate and observe demo robots in pairs. The goal is to experience how communication, timing, and role clarity affect robot operation. This is not a ranking activity; it is a chance to practice working with different people and notice what helps a drive team function well.

## Engineering Notebook Evidence

Students record one contribution they can make, one team behavior they want to practice, partner/role rotation notes, and demo robot design evidence to use later during strategy, robot behavior, and chassis discussions.
