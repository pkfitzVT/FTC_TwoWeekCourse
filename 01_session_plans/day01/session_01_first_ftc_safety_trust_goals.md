## Team Task

Students identify course norms, select initial individual goals, and begin the Demo Robot Design Analysis activity with first rotations focused on safe operation, basic driving feel, chassis shape, controls, first impressions, and partner communication.

## Build / Program / Test Time

Students work in pairs when possible. Each demo robot should have a driver and an operator or observer; if two pairs are assigned to one robot, they take turns operating and filling out the analysis form. Students should record partners and roles.

## Engineering Notebook Evidence

Students record a personal goal, a safety/community commitment, one idea from the SLD, and first demo robot design evidence such as a photo/sketch reference, first driving impression, chassis note, or controls note.
