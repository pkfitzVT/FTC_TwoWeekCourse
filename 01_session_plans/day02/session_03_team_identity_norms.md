## Student-Led Discussion

Run two short SLDs in sequence.

Prompt 1: What norms will help our team include everyone, make decisions fairly, and keep a working robot moving forward?

Prompt 2: What kind of team do we want to become, and what name represents that identity?

## Team Task

Teams use the Team Norms SLD first to choose and document norms, an all-voices plan, a disagreement plan, and a reset/repair plan. Then teams use the Team Identity / Team Name SLD to choose a team identity statement and name connected to the norms. They use the structure/function reading and Demo Robot Design Analysis evidence to explain why robot form should follow strategy. If demo robots are available, teams record or review subsystem design observations before copying the Team Strategy Guide into their portfolio or shared team document and completing the identity, norms connection, and first game-understanding sections. If time allows, they preview the Robot Behavior Specification so they understand that strategy must become specific robot actions before design choices.
