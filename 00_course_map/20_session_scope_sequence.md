# 20-Session Scope and Sequence

This is the current organizing map for the 10-day, 20-session modified FTC robotics course. The website-ready teacher landing page is `docs/teacher_facing_robotics_course_overview.md`, and the individual teacher-facing session pages are in `docs/sessions/`.

| Day | Session | Session title | Main student goal | Main teacher goal | Student-led discussion prompt | Engineering notebook deliverable | Existing materials found in repo | Remaining planning notes |
|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | FIRST, FTC, safety, trust, and personal goals | Understand course community, safety expectations, shared-equipment responsibilities, and personal growth goals. | Establish safety, trust, and stewardship norms. | What do we owe to the teams, mentors, and community members who made this robotics equipment available to us? | Personal goal, safety/community commitment, first SLD note, optional bio. | `02_student_materials/readings/day1_session1_what_is_first.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day1_session1_what_is_first_tech_challenge.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day1_session1_robotics_safety_norms.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day1_session1_borrowed_equipment_expectations.md`; `02_student_materials/decision_menus/day1_session1_individual_goal_menu.md`; `02_student_materials/sld_prompts/day1_session1_sld_shared_materials_student.md`; `docs/sessions/day_01_session_01_first_ftc_safety_trust_goals.md` | Modular FIRST and FTC readings are the source of truth; combined handout archived. |
| 1 | 2 | Collaborative engineering teams | Identify how different strengths and communication styles support a robotics team. | Build low-floor/high-ceiling team culture and participation norms. | How should an engineering team make room for different kinds of contributors? | Teamwork reflection or contribution note. | `02_student_materials/readings/day1_session2_collaborative_engineering_teams.md`; `02_student_materials/engineering_notebook/day1_session1_student_bio_template.md`; `docs/sessions/day_01_session_02_collaborative_engineering_teams.md` | Confirm team formation process. |
| 2 | 3 | Team identity and team norms | Choose a team name and norms using reasons and trade-offs. | Help teams practice low-risk decision-making before robot design. | What should our team name and norms say about the team we want to become? | Team name decision table, team norms table, team agreement. | `02_student_materials/readings/day2_session3_why_team_names_matter.md`; `02_student_materials/decision_menus/day2_session3_team_name_menu.md`; `02_student_materials/decision_menus/day2_session3_team_norms_menu.md`; `docs/sessions/day_02_session_03_team_identity_and_norms.md` | Add final team agreement form if needed. |
| 2 | 4 | First programming session | Connect basic FTC TeleOp code to hardware names, gamepad input, and motor output. | Support first code reading, configuration-name checks, and safe programming evidence. | Why must the names in the configuration file match the names in the program exactly? | Code/config notes and programming portfolio entry. | `02_student_materials/readings/day2_session4_programming_background.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day2_session4_reading_basic_teleop.md`; `docs/sessions/day_02_session_04_first_programming_session.md` | Add device/setup screenshots if needed. |
| 3 | 5 | Chassis design planning and build start | Start chassis assembly from documented design choices. | Manage tools, parts, safety, build sequence, and space constraints. | Which design choice should we protect when the build gets difficult or slow? | SLD decision, chassis sketch, build photos, build log. | `02_student_materials/readings/day3_session5_chassis_physics_space_background.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/principles_of_robot_assembly_day3.md`; `02_student_materials/guides/robotics_design_build_revise_process_handout.md`; chassis decision menus; `docs/sessions/day_03_session_05_chassis_design_planning_and_build_start.md` | Confirm materials checkout and storage routines. |
| 3 | 6 | Chassis continuation and readiness | Continue chassis assembly and inspect before powered testing. | Keep teams focused on alignment, secure fastening, wiring access, and test readiness. | What evidence will show that our chassis is ready for powered testing? | Chassis readiness checklist and issue list. | `02_student_materials/readings/principles_of_robot_assembly_day3.md`; chassis decision menus; `docs/sessions/day_03_session_06_chassis_continuation_and_readiness.md` | Add teacher inspection/approval flow if needed. |
| 4 | 7 | Build drivetrain/chassis | Assemble or complete drivetrain and motor/wheel systems. | Support safe, reliable chassis and drivetrain construction. | What part of our drivetrain is most likely to loosen, rub, or shift during testing? | Build photos, updated sketch, motor/wheel checklist. | `02_student_materials/readings/day4_session7_chassis_build_checklist.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day4_session7_mounting_motors_and_wheels.md`; `02_student_materials/guides/what_should_i_be_doing_right_now_robotics_work_ahead_menu.md`; `docs/sessions/day_04_session_07_build_drivetrain_chassis.md` | Confirm tool stations and drivetrain parts availability. |
| 4 | 8 | Wire, configure, and test drive | Wire drivetrain motors, configure hardware names, and test TeleOp movement safely. | Support first-drive safety and one-change-at-a-time debugging. | What should we change first when the robot does not drive the way we expected? | TeleOp test record, wiring/config table, code notes. | `02_student_materials/readings/day4_session8_wiring_configuration_and_first_drive.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day4_session8_troubleshooting_basic_drive.md`; Day 2 programming readings; `docs/sessions/day_04_session_08_wire_configure_and_test_drive.md` | Add final test-lane/field safety flow. |
| 5 | 9 | Flywheel construction basics | Understand motor power, wheel speed, compression, and launch speed; build first shooter mockup. | Keep shooter work safe, testable, and connected to evidence. | What first shooter variable should our team test, and what evidence will show whether it improved? | Shooter sketch, compression plan, first mockup/build, test hypothesis. | `02_student_materials/readings/day5_session9_flywheel_motor_speed_compression.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day5_session9_flywheel_build_and_test_planning.md`; `02_student_materials/guides/robotics_design_build_revise_process_handout.md`; `docs/sessions/day_05_session_09_flywheel_construction_basics.md` | Confirm safe target/testing area. |
| 5 | 10 | Projectile motion and target behavior | Use launch angle, speed, distance, height, and impact behavior to plan shooter tests. | Help teams reason from projectile evidence instead of random tuning. | What shot path should our team test first, and what miss pattern would tell us what to change? | Shot-test plan and launch reasoning. | `02_student_materials/readings/day5_session10_projectile_motion_for_shooters.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day5_session10_target_impact_and_shot_consistency.md`; `docs/sessions/day_05_session_10_projectile_motion_and_target_behavior.md` | Add shot-test scoring/data sheet if needed. |
| 6 | 11 | Gravity-fed hopper design | Design and hand-test a hopper that feeds balls reliably. | Keep feeding work focused on ball path, jams, and one-ball-at-a-time evidence. | Where is our hopper most likely to jam, and what evidence would prove it? | Hopper sketch/mockup, ball-path test, redesign note. | `02_student_materials/readings/day6_session11_gravity_fed_hopper_design.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day6_session11_hopper_jams_and_ball_path.md`; `docs/sessions/day_06_session_11_gravity_fed_hopper_design.md` | Confirm hopper materials and ball supply. |
| 6 | 12 | Servos and trigger control | Test servo positions and plan a trigger that feeds one ball at a time. | Protect servos from strain and connect mechanism design to code. | What load and release positions should our trigger test first, and how will we know they are safe? | Trigger plan, position values, simple servo test notes. | `02_student_materials/readings/day6_session12_intro_to_servos_positions.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day6_session12_servo_trigger_design_and_testing.md`; `docs/sessions/day_06_session_12_servos_and_trigger_control.md` | Add servo wiring/config screenshots if needed. |
| 7 | 13 | Trigger implementation and first shots | Mount trigger and attempt controlled one-ball shots. | Ensure safe first shots and evidence-based observation. | What pattern did our team notice after several controlled shots? | Mounted trigger photo, shot observation log, code/position notes. | `02_student_materials/readings/day7_session13_trigger_implementation_and_first_shots.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day7_session13_test_shooting_and_observation.md`; `docs/sessions/day_07_session_13_trigger_implementation_and_first_shots.md` | Confirm first-shot station rules. |
| 7 | 14 | Whole-system operation and tuning | Test chassis, hopper, trigger, flywheel, code, battery, wires, and driver actions together. | Help teams tune one subsystem interaction at a time. | Which subsystem worked alone but caused a problem when the whole robot operated together? | Whole-system test log and adjustment record. | `02_student_materials/readings/day7_session14_whole_system_testing.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day7_session14_quick_adjustments_and_tuning.md`; `docs/sessions/day_07_session_14_whole_system_operation_and_tuning.md` | Add reliability test log if needed. |
| 8 | 15 | Robot inspection and readiness | Inspect robot for safety, size, wiring, power, and basic function. | Treat inspection as safety and reliability practice before demonstration. | What inspection issue could make our robot unsafe or unreliable? | Completed readiness checklist and fix/retest notes. | `02_student_materials/readings/day8_session15_robot_inspection_and_safety.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day8_session15_robot_readiness_checklist.md`; `docs/sessions/day_08_session_15_robot_inspection_and_readiness.md` | Confirm final inspection criteria. |
| 8 | 16 | Presentation script and judging prep | Draft a concise team presentation with evidence and award/recognition targets. | Help teams turn portfolio evidence into a clear engineering story. | What should judges remember about our robot and our team after 4 minutes? | 4-minute presentation draft, evidence list, recognition claims. | `02_student_materials/readings/day8_session16_presentation_story_and_script.md`; `02_student_materials/readings/day8_session16_awards_judging_and_growth_narrative.md`; `docs/sessions/day_08_session_16_presentation_script_and_judging_prep.md` | Add final rubric/judge question sheet if needed. |
| 9 | 17 | Practice challenge and evidence collection | Run practice attempts and collect final performance evidence. | Coordinate field use, safety, scoring, and evidence capture. | What does our robot do reliably, and what should we be honest about? | Practice data table, photo/video evidence note, reliability reflection. | `02_student_materials/readings/day2_session4_decode_scoring_strategy_background.md`; `02_student_materials/decision_menus/day2_session4_game_strategy_goals_menu.md`; `docs/sessions/day_09_session_17_practice_challenge_and_evidence_collection.md` | Finalize scoring sheet/challenge rules. |
| 9 | 18 | Presentation planning | Turn notebook evidence into a clear final presentation story. | Support evidence selection, balanced speaking roles, and clear claims. | What story should our team tell about what we built, tested, changed, and learned? | Presentation outline tied to portfolio evidence. | Day 8 presentation readings; engineering notebook; `docs/sessions/day_09_session_18_presentation_planning.md` | Confirm presentation format and timing. |
| 10 | 19 | Final presentation rehearsal | Rehearse final explanation and improve clarity. | Coach concise evidence-based presentation and equitable participation. | How can every teammate contribute to explaining the robot and the process? | Rehearsal feedback notes and final revision list. | Day 8 presentation readings; `docs/sessions/day_10_session_19_final_presentation_rehearsal.md` | Add peer feedback form if needed. |
| 10 | 20 | Final demonstrations and reflection | Demonstrate the robot, present the engineering story, and reflect on growth. | Facilitate final demonstrations, collect evidence, and close the course. | What did we learn about building, programming, testing, documenting, and working as a team? | Final presentation/demo, completed portfolio, final reflection. | `02_student_materials/decision_menus/day1_session1_individual_goal_menu.md`; engineering notebook; `docs/sessions/day_10_session_20_final_demonstrations_and_reflection.md` | Finalize rubric, demo scoring, and equipment return procedure. |
