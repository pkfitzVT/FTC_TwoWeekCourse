Day 9, Session 17: Practice Challenge and Evidence Collection

Purpose

Run practice attempts and collect honest final performance evidence.

Learning Target

Students can collect evidence about what the robot does reliably and what remains limited.

Success Criteria

  • Teams complete practice attempts safely.
  • Teams record results and reliability notes.
  • Teams capture evidence useful for the final presentation.

Teacher Preparation

Prepare field/challenge layout, scoring sheets, batteries, media/evidence expectations, and rotation plan.

Student Background Reading / Preparation

  • Decode scoring strategy background
  • Game strategy goals menu
  • Whole-system testing
  • Robot readiness checklist

Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What does our robot do reliably, and what should we be honest about?

Work Time Options

  • Testing: practice runs or challenge attempts.
  • Driving: repeatable driver/operator routine.
  • Data analysis: score, success rate, failure pattern.
  • Documentation: photo/video evidence note.

Must / Should / Could

  • Must: Run at least one safe practice attempt and record results.
  • Should: Run multiple attempts and identify a reliability pattern.
  • Could: Calculate success rate, cycle time, or compare two driver routines.

Deliverables

  • Practice data table.
  • Photo/video evidence note.
  • Reliability reflection.
  • Engineering portfolio entry.

Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add attempts, results, reliable strengths, limitations, and final improvement decision.

Reflection / Share-Out

  • What should the team be proud of, and what should it be honest about?

Teacher Notes

Keep this framed as evidence collection, not only competition. Use time limits and field rotations to reduce chaos.
