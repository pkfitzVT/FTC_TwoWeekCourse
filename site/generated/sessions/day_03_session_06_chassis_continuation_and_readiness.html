Day 3, Session 6: Chassis Continuation and Readiness

Purpose

Continue chassis assembly and inspect the drivetrain before powered testing.

Learning Target

Students can check whether the chassis is mechanically ready for safe powered testing.

Success Criteria

  • Motors and wheels are secure.
  • Wires and electronics have planned space.
  • Teams identify readiness issues before turning on the robot.

Teacher Preparation

Prepare safe test area, batteries, basic inspection criteria, repair supplies, and storage procedure.

Student Background Reading / Preparation

  • Principles of robot assembly
  • Chassis physics and space background
  • Subsystem design cycle guide
  • Relevant Day 2 chassis decision menus.

Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What evidence will show that our chassis is ready for powered testing?

Work Time Options

  • Assembly: finish drivetrain/chassis structure.
  • Testing: hand-spin wheels, check rubbing, inspect fasteners, and update the frame/drivetrain subsystem cycle.
  • Documentation: readiness checklist and issue list.

Must / Should / Could

  • Must: Complete a mechanical readiness check and frame/drivetrain subsystem test note before powered testing.
  • Should: Fix at least one identified issue and document the change.
  • Could: Create a short inspection routine another team could use.

Deliverables

  • Build photos.
  • Chassis readiness checklist.
  • Issue/fix list.
  • Engineering portfolio entry.

Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add what was checked, what failed readiness, frame/drivetrain subsystem evidence, what was fixed, and what remains.

Reflection / Share-Out

  • What issue did the team catch before it became a powered-test problem?

Teacher Notes

Do not let teams power robots until wheels, motors, battery, hub, switch, and wires are reasonably safe. Frame inspection as normal engineering.
