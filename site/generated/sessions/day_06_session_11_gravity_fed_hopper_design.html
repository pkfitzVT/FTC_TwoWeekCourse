Day 6, Session 11: Gravity-Fed Hopper Design

Purpose

Design and test the ball path that feeds the shooter reliably.

Learning Target

Students can explain how a gravity-fed hopper guides balls into a predictable position before the flywheel launches them.

Success Criteria

  • Teams sketch or mock up a hopper.
  • Teams hand-test the ball path before adding a servo trigger.
  • Teams identify a likely jam or failure point.

Teacher Preparation

Prepare balls, ramp/rail materials, tape or temporary mockup materials, tools, and a test log format.

Student Background Reading / Preparation

  • Gravity-fed hopper design
  • Hopper jams and ball path
  • Subsystem design cycle guide

Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: Where is our hopper most likely to jam, and what evidence would prove it?

Work Time Options

  • Design: start the hopper/artifact holder subsystem cycle with a hopper sketch and ball path.
  • Fabrication: mockup ramp, guide, or rail.
  • Testing: hand-feed multiple balls and observe jams.
  • Documentation: ball-path log.

Must / Should / Could

  • Must: Sketch the hopper, hand-test the ball path, and update the hopper/artifact holder subsystem cycle.
  • Should: Identify one jam point and redesign it.
  • Could: Compare two slopes, openings, or guide shapes.

Deliverables

  • Hopper sketch or mockup.
  • Ball-path testing results.
  • Redesign note.
  • Build photos.

Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add ball path, expected behavior, actual behavior, jam evidence, and next revision.

Reflection / Share-Out

  • How can the hopper help feed only one ball at a time?

Teacher Notes

Require hand testing before servo testing. Treat jams as useful evidence, not failure.
