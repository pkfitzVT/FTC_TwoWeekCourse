Design Menu Template

FTC YES Course

Use this template to create reusable student decision menus. Menus are used before teams make important choices. They provide background information for SLDs, help students compare options, and create evidence for the engineering portfolio.

Menus should help students answer:

What are our options, what does each option help, what does each option make harder, and what evidence will help us decide?
