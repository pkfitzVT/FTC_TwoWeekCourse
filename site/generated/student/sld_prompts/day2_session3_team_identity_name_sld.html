Team Identity / Team Name SLD

Session / Course Phase

Day 2, Session 3: Team identity after team norms

Why This Discussion Matters

Your team name is your first public team identity. It should say something about the kind of team you want to become, not just what sounds interesting in the moment. Use the team norms you just chose to decide what identity your team can actually practice.

SLD is the process. The deliverable is the work product that comes out of the discussion.

Main Discussion Question

What kind of team do we want to become, and what name represents that identity?

What This Discussion Must Produce

By the end of this SLD, record:

  • team identity statement
  • team name
  • reason the name fits the team
  • connection between the name and the team norms
  • optional symbol, color, image, or motto idea

Evidence / Resources to Use

  • Completed team norms
  • Team name menu
  • Team norms menu
  • Student bios or strengths
  • Challenge by Choice expectations
  • FIRST / FTC values
  • SLD tally sheet

Discussion Roles

Role Job
Facilitator Keeps the team focused on identity, not just what sounds cool.
Tally keeper Uses the SLD tally sheet.
Recorder Records name ideas and final choice.
Inclusion checker Asks whether everyone can support the name.

Sentence Stems

  • I want our team to be known for ___.
  • This name fits our team because ___.
  • This connects to our norms because ___.
  • One word I want people to associate with our team is ___.
  • I prefer ___ because ___.
  • A concern I have about this name is ___.
  • I could support this name if ___.
  • This name shows who we want to become because ___.
