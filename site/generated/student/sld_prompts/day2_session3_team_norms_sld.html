Team Norms SLD

Session / Course Phase

Day 2, Session 3: Team formation before strategy and robot design

Why This Discussion Matters

Your team will make many decisions under pressure. Robots break, code fails, parts are limited, and time runs short. Team norms help the group include everyone, disagree respectfully, recover when stuck, and keep a working robot moving forward.

SLD is the process. The deliverable is the work product that comes out of the discussion.

Main Discussion Question

What norms will help our team include everyone, make decisions fairly, and keep a working robot moving forward?

What This Discussion Must Produce

By the end of this SLD, record:

  • 3-5 team norms
  • one plan for including all voices
  • one plan for handling disagreement
  • one reset/repair plan for when the team gets stuck
  • one norm the team thinks will be hardest to maintain under pressure

Evidence / Resources to Use

  • Day 1 shared-materials SLD
  • Team norms menu
  • Student bios, if available
  • Course goals
  • Challenge by Choice expectations
  • SLD tally sheet
  • Personal experience from teams, sports, clubs, robotics, or school projects

Discussion Roles

Role Job
Facilitator Keeps the team on the question.
Tally keeper Uses the SLD tally sheet.
Recorder Writes the norms and decision record.
Equity checker Watches whether all voices are included.

Sentence Stems

  • A norm I think we need is ___ because ___.
  • This would help our team when ___.
  • One problem this norm could prevent is ___.
  • I want to build on ___'s idea by ___.
  • One concern I have is ___.
  • This norm would help include everyone because ___.
  • When we disagree, we should ___.
  • When we get stuck, we should ___.
