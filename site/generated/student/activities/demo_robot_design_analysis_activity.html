Demo Robot Design Analysis

Students should complete this form while operating, observing, or testing a demonstration robot.

The purpose is to collect evidence about different robot designs before making team design decisions. Use these notes later during strategy, robot behavior, chassis, flywheel, hopper, and trigger discussions.

Demo robot analysis creates design evidence. Students use that evidence during strategy, robot behavior, and chassis design discussions.

This activity works best across two sessions. Complete one form for each demo robot you analyze. If there are four demo robots, try to collect evidence from all four before your team finalizes major design choices.

Students should operate demo robots with more than one partner when possible. The goal is to experience how communication, timing, and role clarity affect robot operation. This is not a ranking activity. It is a chance to practice working with different people and to notice what helps a drive team function well.

Rotation Directions

  • Work in pairs when possible.
  • Each robot should have a driver and an operator or observer.
  • If two pairs are assigned to one robot, take turns operating and recording.
  • Record evidence from what you did and what you observed.
  • Rotate partners when possible so you experience different communication styles.
  • Focus on what the design makes easier or harder. Do not rank classmates or drivers.
