# Day 9, Session 18: Presentation Planning

## Purpose

Organize the team's final engineering story around evidence.

## Learning Target

Students can turn notebook evidence into a clear presentation about what they built, tested, changed, and learned.

## Success Criteria

- Presentation outline references real evidence.
- Team members know what they will explain.
- Missing evidence is identified and collected if possible.

## Teacher Preparation

Prepare a presentation template or timing guide, conference schedule, notebook checklist, and peer feedback structure.

## Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What story should our team tell about what we built, tested, changed, and learned?

## Work Time Options

- Presentation: outline and speaker roles.
- Documentation: organize portfolio evidence.
- Media: choose photos, sketches, code, and test tables.
- Reflection: prepare honest limitation and next-step statements.

## Must / Should / Could

- **Must:** Create a presentation outline tied to portfolio evidence.
- **Should:** Assign balanced speaking/evidence roles.
- **Could:** Prepare visual aids or a short live demonstration explanation.

## Deliverables

- Presentation outline.
- Evidence reference list.
- Speaker responsibilities.
- Engineering portfolio update.

## Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add presentation plan, evidence sources, speaker roles, and missing-evidence list.

## Reflection / Share-Out

- What is the strongest evidence for the team's main claim?

## Teacher Notes

Do not let the presentation become a list of parts. Coach teams to explain decisions, failures, revisions, and learning.
