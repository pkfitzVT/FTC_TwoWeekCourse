# Day 10, Session 20: Final Demonstrations and Reflection

## Purpose

Demonstrate the robot, present the team's engineering story, and reflect on growth.

## Learning Target

Students can present what their team built, tested, changed, and learned.

## Success Criteria

- Teams demonstrate safely.
- Teams present evidence-based explanations.
- Students complete final reflection and submit portfolio evidence.

## Teacher Preparation

Prepare demo space, presentation order, reflection form, rubric/scoring tools if used, audience expectations, and equipment return process.

## Student Background Reading / Preparation

- [Individual goal menu](../../02_student_materials/decision_menus/day1_session1_individual_goal_menu.md)
- [Engineering notebook](../../02_student_materials/engineering_notebook/student_engineering_notebook_master.md)
- Day 8 presentation readings.

## Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What did we learn about building, programming, testing, documenting, and working as a team?

## Work Time Options

- Demonstration: robot function.
- Presentation: team explanation.
- Reflection: final individual/team reflection.
- Stewardship: return materials and report damage honestly.

## Must / Should / Could

- **Must:** Present, demonstrate safely, and submit final reflection.
- **Should:** Explain one design decision, one test, one failure, one revision, and one team-growth point.
- **Could:** Connect the project to a future course, career, FTC team, or Super Challenge pathway.

## Deliverables

- Final presentation/demo.
- Completed portfolio submission.
- Final reflection.
- Equipment return notes if needed.

## Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add final reflection, final robot evidence, presentation evidence, and course-growth statement.

## Reflection / Share-Out

- What is one thing the student understands better about robotics?
- What would the team improve with one more week?

## Teacher Notes

Close with appreciation for shared materials and community support. Record equipment issues and improvements for the next course run.
