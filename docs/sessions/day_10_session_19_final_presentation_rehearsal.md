# Day 10, Session 19: Final Presentation Rehearsal

## Purpose

Rehearse final explanations and improve clarity, timing, and participation.

## Learning Target

Students can rehearse a concise, evidence-based explanation of their robot and process.

## Success Criteria

- Teams rehearse within the expected time.
- Students receive and use feedback.
- Every teammate has a way to contribute.

## Teacher Preparation

Prepare rehearsal groups, timing device, feedback form, final checklist, and presentation order.

## Student Background Reading / Preparation

- [Presentation story and script](../../02_student_materials/readings/day8_session16_presentation_story_and_script.md)
- [Awards, judging, and growth narrative](../../02_student_materials/readings/day8_session16_awards_judging_and_growth_narrative.md)
- Engineering notebook evidence.

## Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: How can every teammate contribute to explaining the robot and the process?

## Work Time Options

- Presentation: timed rehearsal.
- Documentation: final revision list.
- Testing: only safety/function checks needed for demonstration.
- Reflection: prepare answers to questions.

## Must / Should / Could

- **Must:** Rehearse once and record feedback.
- **Should:** Revise the presentation based on feedback.
- **Could:** Practice judge-question responses or a smooth robot-demo transition.

## Deliverables

- Rehearsal feedback notes.
- Final revision list.
- Demo readiness checklist.
- Engineering portfolio update.

## Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add feedback received, changes made, final speaking roles, and demo constraints.

## Reflection / Share-Out

- What changed after rehearsal?

## Teacher Notes

Avoid major robot changes unless required for safety or basic function. Protect equitable participation.
