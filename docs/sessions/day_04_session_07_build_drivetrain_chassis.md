# Day 4, Session 7: Build Drivetrain/Chassis

## Purpose

Build or complete the drivetrain and prepare it for wiring and TeleOp testing.

## Learning Target

Students can identify chassis construction vocabulary and assemble a drivetrain with secure motors and wheels.

## Success Criteria

- Motors are mounted firmly.
- Wheels are secure and spin without rubbing.
- Both sides are aligned enough for first testing.

## Teacher Preparation

Prepare drivetrain parts, motor mounts, wheels, hubs, axles, brackets, tools, safety glasses, and build checklist copies.

## Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What part of our drivetrain is most likely to loosen, rub, or shift during testing?

## Work Time Options

- Assembly: mount motors, wheels, hubs, axles, and supports as the current frame/drivetrain subsystem cycle.
- Testing: hand-spin wheels, check rubbing, compare left/right sides.
- Documentation: build photos and updated sketch.

## Must / Should / Could

- **Must:** Mount drivetrain parts securely enough for inspection and update the frame/drivetrain subsystem cycle.
- **Should:** Confirm both sides are built consistently and wheels spin freely.
- **Could:** Diagnose and reduce friction or wobble before powered testing.

## Deliverables

- Build photos.
- Updated chassis/drivetrain sketch.
- Motor/wheel checklist.
- Engineering portfolio entry.

## Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add what was built, what changed from the sketch, frame/drivetrain subsystem cycle evidence, and what still needs checking.

## Reflection / Share-Out

- What part seems most likely to fail or loosen during testing?

## Teacher Notes

Keep students attentive to set screws, hubs, and axle collars. Fast assembly without inspection usually costs time later.
