# Day 8, Session 16: Presentation Script and Judging Prep

## Purpose

Turn engineering evidence into a concise team presentation.

## Learning Target

Students can help draft a 4-minute presentation explaining the robot, design choices, testing, teamwork, growth, and award/recognition targets.

## Success Criteria

- Teams create a clear 4-minute structure.
- Claims are supported by evidence.
- Each member has a meaningful speaking or evidence role.

## Teacher Preparation

Prepare timing expectations, judge-question prompts, notebook access, and an optional presentation conference schedule.

## Student-Led Discussion / Decision

Prompt: What should judges remember about our robot and our team after 4 minutes?

## Work Time Options

- Documentation: select evidence.
- Presentation: draft script and visuals.
- Reflection: growth narrative and award/recognition claims.
- Rehearsal: brief run-through if time allows.

## Must / Should / Could

- **Must:** Draft a 4-minute presentation outline.
- **Should:** Link each main claim to portfolio evidence.
- **Could:** Prepare answers for likely judge questions or a Challenge by Choice explanation.

## Deliverables

- Presentation outline or draft script.
- Evidence list.
- Award/recognition claim table.
- Engineering portfolio update.

## Engineering Portfolio Evidence

Teams should add presentation roles, claims, supporting evidence, judge questions to practice, and growth narrative.

## Reflection / Share-Out

- What is the most important moment in the team's robot story?

## Teacher Notes

Keep presentations concise. Encourage personality and humor only when evidence remains clear.
