# Student Bio Template

## Getting to Know Each Other Before Teams Are Chosen

Before robot teams are assigned, everyone will write a short bio. These bios will help us learn about each other as people, teammates, learners, and future robotics contributors.

You do not need robotics experience to write a strong bio. A good bio helps others understand:

- who you are,
- what strengths you bring,
- what you want to learn,
- how you like to work,
- and how you might help a team.

## What To Do

1. Open a new Google Doc.
2. Title it: **Your Name - Robotics Bio**
3. Copy the template below.
4. Fill it in with honest, complete answers.
5. Review your writing.
6. Paste your finished bio into the shared class bio document.

## Example Bio

**Name:** Maya R.

**Grade:** 10

**Previous experience with robotics, coding, building, tools, design, driving, teamwork, or problem-solving:**  
I have not done robotics before, but I have used basic hand tools at home and I like solving puzzles. I have done some block coding in middle school. I have also worked on group projects where I helped organize the work and keep people on task.

**Strengths I bring to a team:**  
I am patient, careful, and good at noticing details. I usually listen before I talk, and I can help keep track of what the group decided.

**Skills I want to learn or improve during this course:**  
I want to learn how motors and servos work. I also want to get more comfortable with Java code, especially understanding what different sections of the program do.

**Roles I might want to try:**  
I would like to try tester/data recorder, programmer, and portfolio lead. I might also want to try driving once I understand the controls.

**One personal goal I have for this course:**  
I want to become more confident trying technical tasks even when I do not understand everything at first.

**One academic or technical idea I might want to explore:**  
I am interested in collecting data from robot tests and using graphs to decide which design works better.

**One way I can help a team succeed:**  
I can help make sure our team records decisions, test results, and next steps in the engineering portfolio.

**Something teammates should know about how I work best:**  
I work best when I know what job I am responsible for. I also like when people explain why we are doing something, not just what to do.

**A challenge I might face in this course:**  
I might be nervous about speaking up if other people seem more experienced.

**A strategy I can use if I run into that challenge:**  
I can write down my idea first, ask a teammate a question, or volunteer for a specific role like recording data or explaining a test result.

## Before You Paste Your Bio

Check that your bio:

- uses complete sentences,
- gives your teammates useful information,
- includes at least one strength,
- includes at least one goal,
- includes one possible challenge and strategy,
- is respectful and school appropriate.
