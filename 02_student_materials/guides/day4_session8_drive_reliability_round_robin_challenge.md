# Drive Reliability Round-Robin Challenge

## Purpose

Your team will test how well your robot drives when another robot is sharing the course. This is a controlled driving challenge, not bumper cars. The goal is fast, clean, controlled driving.

A robot that works alone may behave differently when another robot is sharing the field. Good drive teams manage speed, space, timing, communication, and recovery from mistakes.

## Readiness Pathways

| Team Status | Session 8 Pathway |
|---|---|
| Robot is not safely driving yet | Wiring, configuration, chassis repair, and first-drive checks |
| Robot drives but is unreliable | Controller mapping, drive power tuning, and practice laps |
| Robot drives reliably | Two-robot round-robin timed challenge |
| Advanced team | Revise tuning, rotate drivers, and improve clean-run score |

Teams enter the challenge when the robot is safe enough to drive on the floor.

## Course Rules

- Two robots start from marked starting zones.
- Both robots follow the same general route or complete the same loop.
- Robots must avoid cones, markers, boundaries, and each other.
- Drivers may need to pass, wait, slow down, or adjust their route.
- The teacher may change the course for space and safety.
- Unsafe driving may result in a reset, penalty, stop, rerun, or disqualification.

## Round-Robin Format

With four teams, use this schedule if time allows:

| Round | Match |
|---|---|
| 1 | Team A vs Team B |
| 2 | Team C vs Team D |
| 3 | Team A vs Team C |
| 4 | Team B vs Team D |
| 5 | Team A vs Team D |
| 6 | Team B vs Team C |

If time is short, use one practice run, one official timed run, and one improvement run if time allows.

## Scoring

```text
final score = time + penalties
```

| Event | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Touches cone / marker / boundary | +5 seconds |
| Needs a reset | +10 seconds |
| Robot-to-robot contact | +10 seconds |
| Unsafe driving | Stop, rerun, or disqualification at teacher discretion |

Positive awards may include fastest clean run, best controlled driving, best driver/operator communication, best improvement between runs, best recovery from a mistake, and best evidence-based adjustment.

## Adjust Between Runs

Between runs, teams should change only one or two things at a time and record what changed.

Possible adjustments:

- drive power
- slow mode
- controller mapping
- driver/operator communication
- starting strategy
- passing or waiting strategy
- wire management
- loose parts
- battery placement
- chassis alignment

## Drive-Team Roles

Competition roles should be based on evidence, fairness, growth, and team needs. The best role plan includes backups.

Use the reusable Robot Operations Role Plan SLD when your team needs a fuller role decision:

`02_student_materials/sld_prompts/reusable_robot_operations_role_plan_sld.md`

Consider:

- Who drives most accurately?
- Who communicates clearly?
- Who stays calm under pressure?
- Who understands the controls?
- Who works well with a partner?
- Who needs practice?
- How will the team include multiple students?
- What happens if the primary driver or operator is absent?
