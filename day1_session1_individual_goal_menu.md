# Individual Goal Menu

## Robotics YES Course

In this course, you will build, test, document, and present an FTC-style robot. You will also practice skills that connect to the BHS Graduate Expectations.

You do not need to be good at everything on the first day. Your job is to contribute, grow, and choose at least one area where you want to stretch.

## What Do Must, Should, and Could Mean?

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Must Do | The expected starting point. Every student should be able to attempt this with support. |
| Should Do | The target level. This means you are taking more ownership and showing growth. |
| Could Do | A stretch challenge. This means you are pushing yourself beyond the minimum in a meaningful way. |

Every student should choose goals from several areas. You should choose at least one **Could Do** goal as a personal challenge.

# Final Reflection Preview

At the end of the course, you will return to these goals and answer:

- What goals did I choose?
- What evidence shows that I worked toward my goals?
- What was my Could-level challenge?
- What obstacles did I face?
- What strategies helped me?
- What did I learn about myself as a teammate, builder, programmer, driver, researcher, presenter, or problem-solver?
- Would I want to continue in robotics, engineering, programming, design, or another related area?
